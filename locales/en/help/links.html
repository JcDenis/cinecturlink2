Books, films, albums, websites you like, shown on the blog by widgets and by a public page.

Links
Click a title to edit the link. Select links to change their rating or category, or to delete them.
Filters
Filter by category or title, and choose the sort order and the number of links per page. The columns are chosen in your preferences.
On the blog
Enable the plugin, choose the folder of images and enable the public page in the blog settings. Add the widgets from the page "Widgets".
